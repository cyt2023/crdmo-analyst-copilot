## Executive Summary

This sample run demonstrates how CRDMO Analyst Copilot supports an end-to-end equity research workflow for the pharmaceutical CRDMO industry. The workflow combines historical sample data analysis, analyst-reviewed assumptions, a simplified DCF model, industry-specific commentary, and exportable report output.

Using the bundled sample dataset, the latest historical year is 2023, with sample revenue of RMB 25,200 million and sample backlog of RMB 30,100 million. Under the default demonstration assumptions, the simplified DCF produces an enterprise value of RMB 79,488.51 million, an equity value of RMB 77,988.51 million, and an implied fair value per share of RMB 26.35.

The main value of the product is not the precision of the sample number. Instead, it is the structured workflow: data intake, historical ratio analysis, human assumption review, CRDMO-specific risk framing, valuation modelling, and report generation.

## Industry Analysis

The pharmaceutical CRDMO industry depends on sustained outsourcing demand from pharmaceutical and biotech innovators, together with the ability of service providers to convert project pipelines into development and manufacturing revenue. In this industry, backlog is an important analytical signal because it provides partial visibility into future work and utilisation.

For a company such as WuXi AppTec, analysts should watch whether order intake remains healthy despite biotech funding volatility and whether backlog quality translates into revenue conversion rather than only headline order visibility. Capacity utilisation is also critical because CRDMO margins tend to be more resilient when facilities and scientific teams are well loaded with commercially relevant projects.

The sector also carries specific risks that can influence valuation beyond near-term earnings. These include regulatory scrutiny, cross-border policy risk, client concentration, pricing pressure during weaker demand periods, and underutilisation risk if new capacity ramps ahead of customer demand.

## Company Analysis

WuXi AppTec is analysed in this project as an integrated CRDMO platform rather than a narrow single-service provider. That matters for valuation because the investment case depends on platform breadth, execution consistency across the outsourcing chain, and the ability to convert customer relationships into repeat business across discovery, development, and manufacturing activities.

In a research workflow, the company should be assessed on backlog quality, margin resilience, cash generation, and exposure to external regulatory or geopolitical issues. The prototype therefore frames the company analysis around operating quality and risk translation rather than around generic high-growth technology language.

## Sensitivity Commentary

The sensitivity analysis confirms that the output is highly dependent on discount rate and terminal growth assumptions. Under this sample scenario, the implied fair value per share ranges from approximately RMB 20.14 to RMB 38.70 across the selected WACC and terminal growth combinations.

This is a useful teaching point for the coursework project because it shows that valuation is not only a calculation problem. It is also an assumption and judgement problem.

## Key Risks

- Data quality risk because the bundled figures are sample prototype data only
- Simplified DCF risk because NOPAT is used as a proxy for free cash flow unless the analyst overrides it
- Regulatory and geopolitical risk affecting cross-border confidence and compliance perceptions
- Demand risk if biotech funding and outsourcing activity weaken
- Utilisation risk if installed capacity grows faster than customer conversion
- Margin risk if pricing pressure increases or service mix deteriorates
- Customer concentration risk if a limited group of clients drives revenue visibility

## Limitations

- The model is designed for coursework demonstration, not live investment decisions
- No real-time market data or live news feed is included
- No automatic source verification is included
- No peer multiple valuation is included in the current version
- The report still depends on human review for data validation and assumption quality

## Suggested Final Coursework Reflection

The strongest feature of this prototype is its industry fit. It uses CRDMO-specific factors rather than treating the company as a generic listed firm. It also demonstrates good workflow logic by separating data analysis, human assumption review, valuation calculation, and risk commentary.

The main area for improvement is source depth. A production-quality version would connect to verified filings, add citations, support scenario analysis, and combine DCF with peer multiple benchmarking.
